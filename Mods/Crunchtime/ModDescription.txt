This mod lets a player enter "Crunch Time", a two-phase income event on themselves. One phase, presented to the player as "Crunch Time", increases their income by a configurable percentage and flat amount for a number of turns; the other, presented as "Rest Time", decreases their income by a separate configurable percentage and flat amount for a number of turns. The player chooses which phase they start with; the other follows automatically afterwards. Once entered it cannot be exited early, and it only runs once per player (one phase, then the other, then it's over).

Phase durations can either be set independently by the map maker, or set to "dynamic", where the player picks a single duration (up to a configured maximum) when they enter - that same duration is then used for both phases.

It can be entered either by playing a card or from the mod menu, chosen by the map maker.
