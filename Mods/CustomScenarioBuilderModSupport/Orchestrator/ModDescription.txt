Lets game creators choose where modded special units and modded custom structures are placed in a custom scenario, so they are on the map from the start of the game.

Use "Planning Mode" (instructions provided) and enter an initial game to export the data and territory placement of structures and special units, then import it in the mod configuration.
Planning mode also comes with convenience options for deleting, cloning and moving structures/special units. As well as placing vanilla structures/built in support special units.

This is a best-effort approach to facilitate mod support in custom scenarios and *CAN NOT* guarantee full support with other mods. 
See the "Mod Support" section in this mod for guidance on levels of support offered.

Testing games to determine mod-compatability is HIGHLY encouraged.
