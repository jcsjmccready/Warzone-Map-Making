This mod places Points of Interest (POI) structures on chosen territories at the start of the game.
There is one structure option for each letter of the NATO alphabet (Alpha to Zulu).

They can be enabled and configured independently to override (No Fog, Light Fog or Fog) lobby fog settings on the territory they are placed on.

Placements are added in the mod config, under Territory POI Placement: pick a POI and type the name of the territory it should be built on when the game starts.
Territories that can not be found at game start will provide an error message to the users.

A convenience option is provided (Planning Mode) that lets you placed POIs in a test game, then load those into the mod for your real game.
