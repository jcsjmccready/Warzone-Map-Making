After a set number of turns, the clock starts: a random (or the biggest) player is handed the Hot Potato, represented by a piece of the Hot Potato card - one piece for every turn it has survived without being passed on. The first time each turn that the current holder wins a fight (winning an attack, and/or successfully defending one, depending on settings) against another player, the Hot Potato - and all its pieces - is passed to the loser of that fight - but it can never be passed straight back to whoever was just holding it.
While the Hot Potato is held, the holder suffers a small income penalty every turn. If it is never passed on for long enough, it explodes: its pieces are taken away, the holder takes a heavy army penalty across all their territories, and the clock resets, counting down again before the next Hot Potato is handed out.
Once too few players remain alive, the Hot Potato stops - any potato in play fizzles out with no explosion, and no new one is handed out.
