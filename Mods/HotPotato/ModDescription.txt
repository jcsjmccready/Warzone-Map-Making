Based on the party game of the same name, the Hot Potato mod is about ensuring you do not hold the Hot Potato by the time the clock runs out.
The clock is indicated through card pieces of the hot potato. Transfering the Hot Potato transfers the pieces. When a player gets the full card, it explodes!

See the configurable options for the penalty for holding the hot potato and for it exploding in your hands.
Only one hot potato can exist at a time.
