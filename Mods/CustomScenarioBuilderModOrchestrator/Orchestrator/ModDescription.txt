Lets game creators choose where modded special units and modded custom structures are placed in a custom scenario, so they are on the map from the start of the game.

Use "Planning Mode" (instructions provided) and enter an initial game to export the data and territory placement of modded structures and special units, then import it in the mod configuration.
Planning mode also comes with convenience options for deleting, cloning and moving structures/special units. As well as placing vanilla structures.

This is a best-effort approach to facilitate mod support in custom scenarios and *CAN NOT* guarantee full support:
- Simple custom structure mods have full-support (structures can always be placed but may have internal tracking data)
- Special unit mods require the mod in question to support this framework*
- Complex custom structure mods require the mod in question to support this framework

*This mod provides built-in support for some older special unit mods. See the "Home" page of this mod and/or glossary for indication.

Testing games to determine mod-compatability is highly encouraged.
